Privacy Policy for Pearl Court EMS

Last updated: May 2026

Pearl Court Estate Management System (“Pearl Court EMS”, “the app”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is designed to support estate administration, resident management, access control, dues tracking, maintenance scheduling, meetings, audit history, and internal communication.

1. Information We Collect

The app may collect and store the following information:

  • Names of users, residents, visitors, and estate personnel
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Apartment or residence information
  • User account information and role assignments
  • Visitor access records and gate logs
  • Estate dues and payment status records
  • Maintenance, activity, meeting, and audit records
  • Internal notification and communication records

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used for:

  • Estate administration and resident registry management
  • Visitor access control and gate security operations
  • Dues tracking, payment status monitoring, and estate accounting
  • Maintenance scheduling and activity tracking
  • User management and role-based access control
  • Audit history, operational logs, and internal notifications

3. Data Sharing

Pearl Court EMS does not sell user data. Data is used only for estate management operations and may be accessed only by authorized estate administrators, security personnel, accountants, or approved users based on their assigned roles.

4. Data Security

The app uses secure cloud services and encrypted network connections where applicable. Data is transmitted over HTTPS between the mobile/web app, backend service, and cloud database.

5. Data Retention

Estate records may be retained for operational, administrative, audit, accounting, and security purposes. Records may be updated or removed by authorized administrators where appropriate.

6. User Access and Data Deletion Requests

Users may request correction or deletion of their data by contacting the estate administrator or app support contact. Requests will be reviewed based on estate operational, legal, accounting, and security requirements.

7. Children’s Privacy

Pearl Court EMS is not designed for children. The app is intended for estate administrators, security personnel, accountants, residents, and authorized adult users.

8. Changes To This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, data access requests, or deletion requests, please contact the Pearl Court EMS administrator.
